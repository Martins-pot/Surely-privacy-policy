3. Device Binding & Management

To protect your account and prevent unauthorised sharing of credentials, Surely implements a device binding system.

What is device binding?
When you register or log in, a unique device identifier is generated and permanently associated with your account on our servers. This identifier — referred to internally as X-Device-Id — is transmitted with every authenticated API request to verify that requests originate from a device you have authorised.

For each registered device we store: the device identifier, the platform, the app version at the time of registration, the timestamp of first registration, and the timestamp of the most recent authenticated request from that device.

Device limit: Each account may have a maximum of two simultaneously bound devices. If you attempt to bind a third device, you will be prompted to remove an existing device from the Manage Devices screen before proceeding.

Behaviour on logout: When you log out, your local authentication token is cleared. Your device identifier and its binding to your account are intentionally retained on our servers. This means that when you log back in on the same device, the existing binding is recognised and no new device slot is consumed. You may permanently remove a device binding via Profile → Manage Devices.

Device removal cooldown: A seven-day cooldown period applies after removing a device, during which no further devices may be removed. The cooldown expiry date is stored locally on your device solely to display the correct date in the app; it is not transmitted to third parties.

Local persistence: Your device identifier and the cooldown expiry timestamp (when applicable) are stored in your device's local secure preferences (Android EncryptedSharedPreferences). This data is not cleared on logout and is cleared only when you uninstall the application or delete your account.

Device binding data is used solely for account security and session management. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or shared with advertising partners.
